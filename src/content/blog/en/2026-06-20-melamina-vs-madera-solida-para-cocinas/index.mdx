When deciding on the cabinets for a custom kitchen, two of the most commonly compared options are melamine and solid wood. Neither is "better" in absolute terms: the right choice depends on your budget, expected maintenance and desired style.

## When melamine makes sense

When you prioritize a controlled budget, simple maintenance, and want to choose from many available colors and finishes.

## When solid wood makes sense

When you're after a specific natural look and are willing to invest in periodic maintenance to keep it that way.

## A middle-ground option

Many projects combine both materials: cabinet bodies in melamine or veneered MDF, with specific details in solid wood (like handles, trim, or a center island) to strike a balance between cost and appearance.
