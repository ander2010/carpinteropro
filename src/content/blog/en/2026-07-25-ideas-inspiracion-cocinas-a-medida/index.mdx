Before settling on the final design for your kitchen, it can help to review a few layout and style
ideas. Here we share some trends we often see in custom kitchen projects.

## Open shelving combined with closed cabinets

Combining open shelving with closed cabinets adds a distinct visual touch without giving up
practical storage for what you'd rather keep out of sight.

## Center island as a work zone

When the space allows it, a center island expands the prep area and can add extra storage or
standing seating.

## Hardware and handles as a design detail

A simple change like the handle or hardware style can define the overall look of the kitchen, from
minimalist to more classic.
