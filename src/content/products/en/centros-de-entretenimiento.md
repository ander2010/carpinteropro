A custom entertainment center is sized to the actual dimensions of your TV, audio equipment and consoles, instead of forcing your equipment into a standard unit.

## What we consider

Ventilation for electronic equipment, a tidy path for cables, and closed storage space for what you'd rather keep out of sight.
