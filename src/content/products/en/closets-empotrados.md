A built-in closet makes use of a full wall in the room, blending visually with the rest of the space without taking up extra volume the way a freestanding wardrobe does.

## Hinged or sliding doors

The choice depends on the space available in front of the closet: sliding doors are ideal when there isn't much room to open hinged doors.
