An integrated kitchen is designed as a whole: upper cabinets, lower cabinets, interior layout and finishes that work together, not as separate pieces.

## What we define in the design

The height and depth of each cabinet, the placement of drawers and organizers, and the finishes that will match the countertop, floor and appliances.
