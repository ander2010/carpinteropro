## Need

The client had a secondary room with no defined use and wanted to turn it into a functional
dressing room, with distinct zones for hanging clothes, folded clothes and accessories.

## Solution

We designed a walk-in closet with double-hang on one side, single-hang and open shelving on the
other, and a center island with drawers for folded clothes and accessories.

## Result

The space went from an unused room to a fully functional dressing room, making use of every
available wall.
