## Need

The original kitchen had deteriorated cabinets and a layout that didn't work well for the client's
daily workflow.

## Solution

We redesigned the entire layout: new upper and lower cabinets, a larger work zone next to the
cooking area, and internal organizers for pots and utensils.

## Result

A kitchen with more usable storage space and a smoother workflow between the prep, cooking and
cleanup zones.
