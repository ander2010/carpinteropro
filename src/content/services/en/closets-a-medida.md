A custom closet is designed around the actual space you have available, not a standard module. That means we can make use of corners, ceiling slopes or columns that an off-the-shelf piece can't handle well.

## How we define the design

Before building anything, we go over how you use the space with you: how much hanging space you need, how many drawers, whether you want a dedicated area for shoes or accessories, and what type of doors you prefer (hinged, sliding or none).

## Materials

We work with different materials depending on budget and use: melamine, veneered MDF or solid wood for specific elements like doors or frames. We walk you through the available options during your project evaluation.
