A custom closet is designed around the actual space you have available, not a standard module. That means corners, ceiling slopes or columns that an off-the-shelf piece can't handle well can be put to good use. Tell us about your project once, and we'll connect your request with a local carpenter who specializes in closets.

## How the design gets defined

Before building anything, the carpenter who contacts you will go over how you use the space with you: how much hanging space you need, how many drawers, whether you want a dedicated area for shoes or accessories, and what type of doors you prefer (hinged, sliding or none).

## Materials

Carpenters in our area work with different materials depending on budget and use: melamine, veneered MDF or solid wood for specific elements like doors or frames. The carpenter will walk you through the available options when they contact you.
