Bathroom furniture faces different conditions than the rest of the house: constant humidity, temperature changes and intense daily use. That's why the material and sealing matter as much as the design. Tell us about your project once, and we'll connect your request with a local carpenter who specializes in this type of furniture.

## What goes into the design

The carpenter who contacts you measures the available space, checks the location of existing plumbing, and works with you to define how much storage capacity you need for towels, toiletries and other accessories.
