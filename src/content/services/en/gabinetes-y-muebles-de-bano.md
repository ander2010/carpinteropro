Bathroom furniture faces different conditions than the rest of the house: constant humidity, temperature changes and intense daily use. That's why the material and sealing matter as much as the design.

## What we consider in the design

We measure the available space, the location of existing plumbing, and define with you how much storage capacity you need for towels, toiletries and other accessories.
