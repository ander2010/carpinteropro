A poorly installed door shows quickly: rubbing, forced closures or uneven gaps. The carpenter who contacts you takes care with the frame and leaf adjustment so the door works correctly from day one. Tell us about your project once, and we'll connect your request with a local carpenter.

## What gets checked

The carpenter verifies the opening is square, adjusts hinges and hardware, and tests the final closure before considering the job done.
