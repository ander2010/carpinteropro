Not every space fits the furniture you find in stores. When a space has atypical measurements, or you simply want something that doesn't exist as a standard product, custom furniture solves the problem. Tell us about your project once, and we'll connect your request with the right carpenter in your area.

## Example projects

Shelving for narrow or uneven spaces, wall-integrated desks, entertainment centers sized to the actual TV and furniture, or one-off pieces to complete a room — all projects carpenters in our area have built to measure.
